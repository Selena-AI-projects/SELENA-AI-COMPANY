## Platform

web

## Users

Selena Systems serves growing businesses that need either a measurable AI
visibility product, a custom AI system, or practical research and education.
The master brand must make those paths easy to distinguish without creating
separate companies or identities.

## Product Purpose

Selena Systems has two commercial directions and one shared intellectual layer:

1. AI Visibility measures and improves AI visibility.
2. AI Automation designs and implements custom operating systems and workflows.
3. Selena Lab publishes research, experiments, guides, articles and, later,
   separately purchased courses.

One Selena identity serves Visibility, learning and service projects through
independent entitlements.

## Positioning

Selena Systems helps businesses become visible to AI and automate real operating
workflows. AI Visibility keeps Visitor View and API View separate, records
every run in an Evidence Ledger and ties recommendations back to observable
evidence. Selena Lab is the supporting research and education engine, not a
third competing service line.

## Operating Context

- `selenasystems.com` is the public marketing and free-readiness surface.
- `app.selenasystems.com` is the authenticated client workspace.
- `/lab` is the public research and education library; there is no separate Blog
  or Academy.
- `/app/learn` is reserved for future course entitlements; `/app/selena` remains
  the Visibility workspace and `/app/projects` the custom-services workspace.
- Visitor View covers ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity.
- API View covers Claude, DeepSeek, Qwen, Mistral and Grok with web search off by
  default.
- Public website readiness can run without connecting private client accounts.
- Paid AI measurements require an approved order, fixed cardinality and provider
  budget caps.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The immutable RC6 contract keeps stable plan IDs and the internal names Visitor
Local, Full AI Landscape, Expert Verified and Growth 90 Days. The final
customer-facing ladder presents the same scopes as:

- AI Visibility Snapshot — $49/month; up to 25 questions in one language; three
  Visitor View systems; one weekly run; up to 300 planned answers per month.
- AI Visibility Landscape — $79/month; up to 25 questions across up to two
  languages; all eight systems; one weekly run; up to 800 planned answers per month.
- Expert Verified — $399 one-time; 25 questions in each of two languages; all
  eight systems; five repeats; 2,000 planned answers plus human semantic,
  citation and factual QC.
- Implementation + 90 days — $2,490; manual approval and an immutable custom scope.

AI Automation has four manually approved formats: a $100 60-minute mini-audit,
a $500 AI Audit, a $4,500 four-week AI Sprint and an eight-week AI Business OS
starting at $10,000.

Online checkout, live payments, recurring maintenance and real provider calls
remain off until their separate owner gates and production acceptance are
complete. Google Places is excluded from the MVP. Connected Analytics is
optional and requires explicit account authorization.

The older `$9 / $500 / $4,000 / from $10,000` Visibility ladder in historical
architecture documents is superseded for this product surface by the RC6 catalog
above. Other Selena Systems consulting offers remain separate services.

## Brand Commitments

AI Visibility is a product line inside Selena Systems, not a separate
company. The public voice is clear, practical, evidence-first and avoids hype,
fake urgency and unverifiable claims. The current public seller source of truth
is `Selena Systems LLC`, United States, as defined in `lib/commercial-facts.ts`.

## Evidence on Hand

- A live public website-readiness check and sample report exist in this repo.
- RC6 v1.2 was accepted in staging in the separate AI Visibility application.
- The Usha pilot includes a deterministic 240-row Evidence Ledger and client PDF.
- No public customer testimonials or generalized performance claims are approved;
  future work must not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

1. Evidence before interpretation.
2. Visitor View and API View remain visibly separate.
3. No measurement or spend before an explicit approved boundary.
4. Automated findings state their limits; Expert Verified requires a human QC
   record.
5. Public checks provide value before login or payment.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The web experience must meet WCAG AA contrast, keyboard navigation and 44×44 px
minimum touch targets. English and Russian product routes must stay equivalent
in meaning and commercial scope.
